# I Stopped Doing the Work That Wasn't the Work

---

Applied to 40+ companies. Updated my tracker exactly 0 times.

I land on a job listing → one click → it's logged.
A rejection lands in my Gmail → my spreadsheet already knows.
I open my laptop in the morning → everything is current.

I never opened a spreadsheet to type anything.
I never checked Gmail to update a status.
I never applied to the same role twice.

I just focused on preparation.

That's what happens when you stop managing the process and build something that manages it for you.
